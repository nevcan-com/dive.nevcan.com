# GUE logos

Vector artwork taken unaltered from GUE's Brand Standards Guide v2.0 (2026) (in the GUE hub:
`PDFs/official-docs/GUE-Brandbook-v2.pdf`); only the guide's grey backing squares were removed.
Trademark of Global Underwater Explorers. As a GUE instructor, Nev may use them to promote his
GUE courses and services (guide, "Permissions"), never for non-GUE training or projects.

| File                       | Version                                  | Use                                                |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| `gue-logo-color-dark.svg`  | full colour, white text, no motto (p. 9) | on the site now (`public/media/gue-logo-dark.svg`) |
| `gue-logo-white.svg`       | single-colour white, no motto (p. 10)    | small sizes on dark backgrounds                    |
| `gue-logo-white-motto.svg` | single-colour white, with motto (p. 10)  | large sizes only, where the motto reads            |

Rules from the guide: never redraw, recolour or distort; no drop shadow; an even background with
enough contrast (not over a busy photo); clear space of at least 1.3× the logo's size; no motto in
small applications; don't combine it with other logos without GUE's permission. The horizontal and
block-letter logos (p. 11) are for official GUE sites and licensed apparel, so they are not here.
To use one on the site, copy it to `public/media/` and credit it in `CREDITS.md`.
