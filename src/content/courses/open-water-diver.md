## Why this course

Most entry-level courses teach you to survive a dive. GUE Open Water teaches you to dive well:
the same configuration, procedures and team habits that GUE's technical and cave divers use,
scaled to your first dives. You learn buoyancy and trim before anything else, so the reef, the
wreck and the visibility stay as you found them.

It is the course I recommend to anyone starting out. One course, five days, and you leave as an
independent diver with a foundation you will never need to unlearn.

## How the days run

A typical flow; we adapt it to the group and the conditions.

1. **Day 1:** Theory, equipment set-up, first pool session
2. **Day 2:** Pool sessions: breathing, buoyancy, trim, mask and regulator skills
3. **Day 3:** Pool sessions: gas sharing, ascents, rescue basics; first open water dives
4. **Day 4:** Open water dives: navigation, SMB, team procedures
5. **Day 5:** Final open water dives, review and debrief
