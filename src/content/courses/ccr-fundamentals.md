## The step onto a rebreather

CCR Fundamentals is where GUE divers move from open circuit to closed circuit. It builds on
Technical Fundamentals: the same trim, buoyancy and team procedures, now with a loop to manage,
a bailout plan, and a new set of physiological limits to respect.
