## Why this course

Technical Fundamentals is taught in doubles, and it asks for experience in them: 25 dives in a
double tank configuration, or 15 after a Doubles Primer. If you have never dived a twinset, this
day is the shortest way in. You arrive at Fundamentals already balanced in the set, and spend
the course on skills instead of fighting the equipment.

## How the day runs

1. **Morning:** The double tank configuration: fit, assembly, hose routing, gas analysis
2. **Midday:** Land drills: GUE EDGE, pre-dive sequence, valve drill
3. **Afternoon:** Two dives: buoyancy and trim, valve drill, long hose deployment
