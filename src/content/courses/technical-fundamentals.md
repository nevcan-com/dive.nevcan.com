## Why this course

GUE has three fundamentals courses, and they cover the same ground to different depths:
Performance Diver in two days, Basic Fundamentals in three, Technical Fundamentals in four.
Technical Fundamentals is the complete one. It is taught in doubles, to the tightest standard of
the three, and it is the only one that opens GUE's technical, cave and rebreather courses:
Technical Diver 1, Cave Diver 1 and CCR Fundamentals all require it.

If you already dive, and you can see yourself going further one day, this is the course I
recommend. Four full days take you from good to precise, and you will not need to come back
for an upgrade later.

## How the days run

A typical flow; we adapt it to the team and the conditions.

1. **Day 1:** Equipment configuration, land drills, first dives: trim, buoyancy, propulsion
2. **Day 2:** Propulsion techniques, the valve drill and S-drill, gas sharing
3. **Day 3:** Lights, touch contact, SMB deployment, ascents with stops
4. **Day 4:** Rescue skills, full scenarios, final dives and debrief

Theory runs online before and during the course, with sessions on site.
