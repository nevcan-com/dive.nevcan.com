## The project

Büyük Mağara, in Samandağ, was found by Mahmut İğde in May 2017. I first dived it in 2021, with
Mahmut as our guide, and that dive gave us enough data to start thinking about a map. In 2024 I
began the survey: over several dives we laid a guideline into the cave and documented the work in
photographs and video. The region holds several more caves.

## Where it stands

Part of the cave has been surveyed, and I am analysing the data; the survey is not complete yet,
and the work continues. Next is photogrammetry, to add a 3D record of the cave to the map. The
map, and what it shows, will be published here when it is finished.
