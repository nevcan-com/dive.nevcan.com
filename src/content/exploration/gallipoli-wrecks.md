## The park

The Çanakkale Wars Gallipoli Historical Site Directorate, part of Türkiye’s Ministry of Culture
and Tourism, opened the Gallipoli Historical Underwater Park on 2 October 2021, at Seddülbahir
Castle: the first underwater park in the world themed on the First World War. It opened with 14
dive points. By June 2026 it listed 29, on 27 historic wrecks and two natural reefs, at depths from
a few metres to beyond 80. More than 100 wrecks from the war have been recorded in these waters.

## Protecting the wrecks

Steel hulls more than a century old are corroding. In June 2026 the Directorate and the 1915
Çanakkale Bridge and Motorway Corporation launched Deep Heritage (Derin Miras): documenting and
digitally recording the wrecks, fitting them with cathodic protection, and developing diving on
them sustainably. The anodes are expected to add 30 to 35 years to a wreck’s life. HMS Louis, off
Suvla, was the first to receive them.

The Directorate’s teams also recover and conserve artefacts from the wrecks; the navigation lamp
raised from HMS Triumph, at 72 metres, is to be shown in its museums.

## My part

This is the Directorate’s work, not a project of my own. I take part in dives on these wrecks as a
safety diver and dive guide when dive groups come to the wrecks. At Gallipoli Wreck Fest in 2025, the
park’s international underwater photography competition, I was called in as boat and safety
supervisor. My aim is to contribute, as a technical diver, to the
research and documentation of these wrecks.
