## The project

Akdeniz University’s underwater archaeology team has surveyed Türkiye’s Mediterranean coast
since 2000, with permits from the Ministry of Culture and Tourism. The surveys have identified
282 shipwrecks along the coasts of Antalya, Mersin, Hatay and Adana, and the team now excavates
several of them under the ministry’s Heritage for the Future programme.

The work is led by Assoc. Prof. Dr. Hakan Öniz, who heads the university’s Underwater
Archaeology Research Center and its graduate programme in Mediterranean underwater cultural
heritage.

## My part

I join the team as a volunteer project diver. Several of these wrecks lie deeper than
recreational depth limits; I dive them as the team’s technical diver, trained and certified
with GUE. Off Fethiye in 2025, I located two of the four new wrecks the team found. Underwater, I help with the
research and the excavation, take photographs, and document finds for the inventory. The
science, and every conclusion drawn from it, belongs to the research team.
